FAQ

Here's a list of questions i'm asked often. This is not an About Me, so it will not always include a lot of context.

Why do you know so much about goats? Why is your branding goat/themed?

There's no secret deeper meaning. Goats are my favorite animal, and i've loved Greco-Roman Mythology since the fourth grade, so I used a satyr/faun in my online branding. Some of the things I like about goats specifically are their playful and chaotic demeanor, their cleverness and curiosity, and how many weird litle quirks are unique to just them as a species.

Why do you need a website? What's the point of this site?

Mostly for fun and to learn web development. This site is also currently used to host Pathfinder 2e sessions, stream media, send emails, and to self-host a rotating selection of apps for myself.

Why is this website frameworkless?

This site's a place for me to learn. I feel that a frameworkless aproach is the best way for me to learn baseline webdev skills. However, if I ever feel I've learned enough this way, I'll switch this site over to a framework.
